Unit 2 Media Revision: Match the Term

Listen, watch, then find the psychology hiding in it.

How it works

  1. Listen to the song or watch the clip first.
  2. Each card shows a lyric or a scene, plus a definition. In lyrics, the key term is blanked out.
  3. Drag a term from the bank into the box next to its definition. On a phone or iPad, tap a term, then tap the box.
  4. Tap a placed term to send it back. Some terms in the bank are extras that don’t fit anywhere.
  5. Press Check when you’re done. Fix the ones marked ✗ and check again.
